Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

Summary for Busy Minds

Minimal Diary is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy. All your journals, habits, mood logs, and photos stay exclusively on your device. We do not operate user accounts, we do not track you, and we never collect or transmit your personal data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike many productivity apps, Minimal Diary functions without a centralized backend server. We do not collect:

  • No names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No journal entries, notes, habits, or mood logs.
  • No location or GPS data.
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs.
  • No third-party analytics or behavioral tracking data.

2. On-Device Data Storage

All data you create in Minimal Diary is stored exclusively in your device's local application sandbox via iOS / Android secure local storage. If you delete the app or clear its data, all stored entries are permanently erased from your device unless you have made a backup.

3. Device Permissions (Photos & Media)

Minimal Diary requests access to your photo library only when you choose to attach a daily memory photo to a journal entry. When granted:

  • The photo you select is copied directly into the app's local storage sandbox.
  • Your photos are never transmitted over the internet or uploaded to any server.
  • You can revoke photo library permissions at any time via your device's Settings.

4. Data Backups & Export

You have full ownership of your data. Minimal Diary provides built-in tools allowing you to:

  • Export your journals and entries into an offline, human-readable PDF booklet.
  • Create a full data archive (ZIP/JSON) to store on iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or transfer to another device.

Minimal Diary never receives a copy of your exported backups.

5. Children's Privacy

Minimal Diary does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It is completely safe and compliant with privacy standards such as COPPA and GDPR.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted here with an updated "Effective Date".
