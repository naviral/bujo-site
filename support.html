Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does Bujo require an internet connection?

No. Bujo is 100% offline-first. All your notes, daily logs, habits, and photos are saved directly on your device. You can journal on flights, in the wilderness, or anywhere without cell reception.

How do I backup my data?

Open Bujo, tap the menu/settings icon in the top navigation bar, and select "Backup & Export". You can generate a complete ZIP archive or export your entries as a clean, printable PDF booklet.

How do I restore data on a new device?

In the "Backup & Export" menu on your new device, select "Restore Data" and choose your previously exported ZIP archive. All your entries, habit history, and photos will be restored automatically.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

Never. When you add a daily photo to your journal, a copy is stored in Bujo's local sandboxed folder on your device. We do not have servers, and we never view or transmit your photos.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual entries anytime directly within the app. If you wish to completely wipe all data, you can use the "Reset Data" option in settings or uninstall the app from your device.
